  Local listener: System (PID 4), IPv4 0.0.0.0:445 and IPv6 [::]:445

Interpretation and limitations
------------------------------
An open port means a local application accepted Nmap's probe on loopback.
It does not prove that the service is reachable from another machine; host
firewall rules, interface binding, routing, and network profile affect that.
Process ownership was checked locally with Get-NetTCPConnection and
Get-Process. No exploitation, vulnerability scripts, credentials, or external
targets were used. Review the saved command output in scan_output\.
